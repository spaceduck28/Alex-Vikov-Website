Services

Four ways to work with me. One goal: yours.

Pick the format that fits the season you're in. If you're not sure, that's exactly what the discovery call is for, we'll figure it out together.

01
For individuals

1-on-1 Coaching

Your money. Your goals. A plan built around both.

The deepest, most personal work I offer. We meet on a regular cadence, build a real plan around your real life — income, debts, goals, family, the lot — and execute it together with the kind of accountability you can't get from a book or a podcast.

This is for the professional who's tired of vague advice and ready to make decisions with full clarity on what they cost and what they buy.

  • Fully personalized financial plan, refined as your life changes
  • Regular sessions — weekly, biweekly, or monthly (we decide together)
  • Direct access to me between sessions for the moments that matter
  • Goal tracking, behavior coaching, and honest course-corrections

02
For cohorts

Group Coaching

Learn alongside professionals on the same path.

Same frameworks, smaller investment, and the energy of a cohort that's solving the same problems you are. There's a particular kind of momentum that only happens when you're not the only one doing the work — and group coaching is built around that.

Best for professionals who learn faster with peers and want a structured curriculum to move through together.

  • Small cohort of 8–12 motivated professionals
  • Live group sessions on a fixed schedule
  • Workbook, templates, and a structured curriculum
  • Private community for accountability between sessions

03
For companies

Enterprise Coaching

Give your team financial confidence. Watch retention follow.

Financially stressed employees are distracted employees. Studies have shown they make slower decisions, take more sick days, and leave for the next job that pays a few thousand more. A workforce that feels in control of its money is the highest-ROI benefit you can offer.

I build financial wellness programs custom-fit to your company — practical, jargon-free, and designed to actually move the needle on the metrics you care about.

  • Programs custom-built to your industry, salary bands, and benefits
  • Live workshops, recorded sessions, or hybrid delivery
  • Optional 1-on-1 advisory access for key team members
  • Pre/post measurement to show real outcomes to leadership

Bring real financial wisdom to your stage.
