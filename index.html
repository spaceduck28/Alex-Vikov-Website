The Story

I learned the hard way. You don't have to.

"Every lesson I teach is one I paid for in years I won't get back."
MBA, University of Tokyo Registered Financial Planner

By my mid-thirties, I had a respectable career, a steady paycheck, and almost nothing in the bank. It wasn't because I wasn't earning. It was because nobody had ever taught me what to do with what I earned.

The lessons that quietly compound into freedom — how to plan, how to invest, how to make money work while you sleep — I learned a decade too late. I watched friends with half my income retire on their own terms while I scrambled to catch up.

So I went and got the education I wished I'd had earlier. An MBA from the University of Tokyo. A Registered Financial Planner certification. And, more importantly, real-world reps applying it to my own life until the math worked.

Today I coach professionals who are exactly where I once stood — talented, capable, and quietly worried they're already behind. They're not. They just don't have a plan yet. That's where I come in.
